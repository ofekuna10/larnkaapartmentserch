Provenance

Geometry is colour-traced from the raster plan sheets, then scaled by the dimensions printed on each sheet. Openings come from the glazing strips the plans draw; interior doorways are left as gaps. Furniture sits on the footprints the plans draw, at standard heights, in neutral tones — the volumes are the point, and the plan overlay carries the real finishes. Areas are measured off the trace, not quoted from the brochure.
